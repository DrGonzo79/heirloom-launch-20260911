# Phased build prompts

## Phase 1 — Versioned owner intake

“Add authenticated recipe-owner intake with separate story and formulation permissions, recipe-version hashes, contributor approvals, allergen fields, and explicit export logs. Keep the current deterministic screen. Test blank/duplicate contributors, revoked approval, stale browser state, interrupted save, and unauthorized reads.”

## Phase 2 — Verified production discovery

“Add a manufacturer registry with capability evidence, verification timestamps, MOQ tiers, lead-time ranges, geography, certifications, and contact outcomes. Build a human verification queue and expire unsupported claims. Match process needs, not category labels alone. Test zero matches, stale evidence, conflicting claims, duplicate outreach, timeout, and opt-out.”

## Phase 3 — Compliance and local pilot

“Add process-authority, shelf-life, allergen, label, insurance, and ownership gates with named responsible reviewers. Build a 100-unit pre-sell and local-retail pilot ledger for orders, samples, weekly sell-through, returns, and reorder. No production approval while any hard gate lacks evidence.”

## Phase 4 — Evidence-gated studio

“Only after three unrelated products each earn two reorders, add shared procurement, packaging templates, retailer CRM, inventory exposure limits, owner approval workflows, and transparent revenue-share statements. Instrument product contribution after all landed costs. Keep portfolio acquisition and national paid growth deferred until shared operations improve margin.”
